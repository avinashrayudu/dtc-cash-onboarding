## Model

- Trained on 7 brands, 2606 labeled bank and card lines, 176 accounts.
- Leave-one-brand-out accuracy without abstaining: lines 96.2%, accounts 94.9%.
- Abstain threshold chosen for 99% precision on held-out brands: lines 0.49, accounts 0.5.
